Important Health & Caloric Deficit Advisory:

Targeting a 0.65 lbs/day loss equates to roughly 4.55 lbs/week, demanding a severe daily caloric deficit of approximately ~2,275 kcal/day (since 1 lb of body mass represents ~3,500 calories). Standard medical guidelines typically recommend 1–2 lbs/week. A rapid pace carries risks of lean muscle loss, micronutrient depletion, gallstones, and metabolic fatigue. Prioritize adequate hydration, adequate protein (0.8–1g/lb of lean mass), electrolyte balance, and consult a qualified medical physician or registered dietitian before initiating an intense restriction.
